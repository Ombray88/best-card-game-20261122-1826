An original, turn-based card duel game where you battle an intelligent AI opponent in mystical elemental combat. Built fresh with robust offline architecture and deterministic shot staging.

## How to Play
- **Objective**: Reduce the opponent's life from 30 to 0 before they deplete yours. First to 0 life loses.
- **Ember (Mana)**: Each turn you gain +1 ember (max 10) and draw a card from your deck.
- **Cards & Elements**: Cards cost ember and feature attack values and elemental suits (Flame 🔥 / Red Triangle, Tide 💧 / Blue Circle, Leaf 🍃 / Green Diamond, Stone 🪨 / Brown Square — distinguished by color and unique symbols). Some cards also feature special effects like healing, extra card draw, or shield guarding.
- **Controls**: 
  - Click a card in your hand to select it, then click your target (the opponent directly, or their played guard card).
  - Click "End Turn" when you are done playing cards.
  - Keyboard shortcuts: Enter/Space to start or confirm actions, Esc to pause.
